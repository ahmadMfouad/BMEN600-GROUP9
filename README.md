## Candidate Project 1

### Biomedical Problem
ECG software gives a diagnostic reading before a cardiologist sees the recording. ECGs differ between men and women, and some diagnostic criteria were built without separating sexes.

### Possible Research Question
Where do automated ECG diagnoses disagree with cardiologist annotation, and does the disagreement cluster by sex or age? (Compare)

### Dataset
PTB-XL and PTB-XL+ (PhysioNet, open, CC BY 4.0). 21,799 12-lead ECGs read by cardiologists, with automatic 12SL diagnoses for the same records. Sex, age, and signal quality included.
https://physionet.org/content/ptb-xl/1.0.3/
https://physionet.org/content/ptb-xl-plus/1.0.1/

### Biggest Uncertainty
Some PTB-XL labels are autogenerated rather than cardiologist-validated. If too few are validated, we are comparing machine to machine.

## Candidate Project 2

### Biomedical Problem
Falls are the leading cause of injury death in adults over 65. Men and women fall at similar rates, but women are injured more often.

### Possible Research Question
Among older adults who fall, what predicts whether the fall causes injury, and does it differ by sex? (Associate)

### Dataset
BRFSS (CDC), public, no registration. Asks how many falls in the past 12 months and how many caused injury, plus age, sex, health status, and activity.
https://www.cdc.gov/brfss/

### Biggest Uncertainty
The falls questions aren't in every survey year. Data is self-reported by phone and excludes nursing home residents.
